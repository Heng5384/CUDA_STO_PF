# Effective 6 h fixture derivation

The experimental matrix anchor is xAg=0.0062, converted with
xB=2*xAg/(2-xAg) to xB=0.006219279767278563. With mean C_B_tot=0.03 and
v_B=1, the registered diffuse beta fraction is
0.02392954476632684.

The fixture uses a deterministic 4 by 4 by 2 periodic lattice in 128^3 and
32 resolved particles. The three-radius ladder is 0.9/1.0/1.1 times a
common scale; the scale is solved against the sum of h(phi)*dV, not against
the sharp sphere volume. The smallest periodic separation margin is positive
(0.214 nm). This is a coarse-grained validation population, not one seed per
APT Ag-isoconcentration object.
