`BLOCKED_PROFILE_EQUILIBRATION_METHOD` remains the clean-branch decision: V2-A
is frozen historical evidence, while the clean zero-mode runtime cannot load a
locally equilibrated profile.  The isolated profile-extension source now has a
completed 96^3, three-particle A/B/C comparison (see
`multi_particle_v2_results_v1.md`) with particle identity and continuous/
restart checks (see `multi_particle_v2_restart_validation.md`).  This closes
the validation-only multi-particle gate and supports a bounded profile-vs-
elasticity diagnostic; it does not make a clean production or publication
claim, and the historical V2-A 6–12 h trajectory must not be relabeled as
V2-B/C.
