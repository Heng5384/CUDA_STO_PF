# Nonelastic finite-particle method

Use resolved radii 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16 and 20 nm only where the periodic box and interface resolution are valid. For each radius bracket the sign of `dR/dt` at xAg=0.0046541, 0.0058169, 0.0062193 and 0.03. Stop once the sign is unambiguous; store scalar diagnostics and sparse fields only.
