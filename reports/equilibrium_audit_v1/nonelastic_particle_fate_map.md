# Nonelastic particle fate map

The available workstation windows provide bounded, not equilibrium, fate
observations.  R=8 nm has a negative low-composition long window and a
short-window positive high-composition response; the remaining R=6, 10, 12,
14, 16 and 20 nm entries are short post-relaxation observations.  They must not
be promoted to zero-growth roots because finite diffuse interfaces and
conserved PF composition alter the dynamic growth sign.  CNT critical radii
alone are insufficient for this classification.
