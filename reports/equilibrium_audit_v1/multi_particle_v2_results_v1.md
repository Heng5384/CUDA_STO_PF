## Important fixture correction

The first B/C attempt (`*_96_v1`) is intentionally retained but excluded from
the result.  Its VTK-to-raw conversion used C-order instead of the runtime's
`k,j,i` VTK convention and transposed one seed.  The corrected materializer
uses Fortran-order VTK reconstruction; all reported v2 runs use that corrected
field.  This is why the report contains both v1 (negative diagnostic) and v2
(authoritative extension) roots without overwriting either.

## Interpretation

The multi-particle V2 execution and restart gates pass for the isolated
extension.  It establishes a short-time profile-vs-elasticity comparison and
stable particle identities.  It does not establish a planar chemical/coherent
equilibrium root, and it cannot be promoted to clean production evidence until
the fresh-profile extension is separately reviewed or merged into a qualified
branch.
