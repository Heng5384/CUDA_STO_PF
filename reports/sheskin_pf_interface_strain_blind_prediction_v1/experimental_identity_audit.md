# Experimental identity audit

`PASS_EXPERIMENTAL_IDENTITY_SEPARATION`

- Figure 5c: measured total thermal conductivity.
- Figure 7 open symbols: Wiedemann-Franz-derived lattice thermal conductivity.
- Figure 7 filled symbols: Wiedemann-Franz-derived electronic thermal conductivity.
- Yu curves: calculated Debye-Callaway quantities and not Sheskin measurements.
- PF curves: calculated full-PSD quantities and not Sheskin measurements.

Only Figure 5c measured-total values enter the experimental CSV. Because the electronic term is roughly 1e-4 of the lattice term, measured total is a numerically adequate target at the retained 0.015 W m^-1 K^-1 digitization resolution, but its identity is never renamed. TEM/APT and SE/FIB number densities are kept separate because they use different detection scales and may overlap in object identity.
