# CNT Energy Curves With Critical-Radius Markers

- Each `TxxxC_energy_curves_with_critical_radius` figure contains all five xB conditions at that temperature.
- Blue circles: final values from the original energy-minimization radius scan.
- Grey square: same-strain r=0 matrix reference, plotted as zero after reference subtraction.
- Orange dashed line: local five-point quartic fit around the discrete barrier peak.
- Red star and vertical dotted line: `r*_fit` used by the postprocess table.
