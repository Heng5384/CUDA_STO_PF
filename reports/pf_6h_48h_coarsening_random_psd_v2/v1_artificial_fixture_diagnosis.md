# V1 artificial-fixture diagnosis

The V1 fixture used a 128^3 periodic domain with centers on the exact 4x4x2
Cartesian lattice. Centers repeated at x/y coordinates 16, 48, 80, 112 nm
and z coordinates 32, 96 nm. The 32 seeds therefore form one translational
orbit repeated across the box, rather than a statistically representative
population.

The radius table had only three repeated classes: 6.3, 7.0, and 7.7 nm. The
periodic separation audit reported a minimum margin of only 0.214 nm after
the required 4*lambda clearance.

The observed resolved count was 32 at the raw fixture, 24 at 7 h, 16 at 8 h,
and 8 at 9 h. The eight survivors had identical radii and positions within
the repeated lattice orbit and remained unchanged through 48 h. This exact
halving sequence is a fixture-symmetry signature, not evidence of a broad
physical population.

The 8 h matrix xAg peak was 0.0068898. It coincided with a transient beta
volume-fraction decrease from 0.0239295 to 0.0232535 before the 9 h survivor
state recovered to approximately 0.02424. The V2 fixture removes the spatial
and radius symmetries without changing thermodynamics or kinetics.
