## Net result

V3 converts a “bounded insufficiency” endpoint into a positive, mechanism-layered story without hiding the residual. The central upgrade is not stronger adjectives; it is a new causal organization of existing evidence: observation gap justifies ensembles, random realization selects kinetic paths, and interfacial area controls the leading modeled thermal response. Claims dependent on production authority, observation identity, thermodynamic identity, finite size or designed controls remain explicitly downgraded.
