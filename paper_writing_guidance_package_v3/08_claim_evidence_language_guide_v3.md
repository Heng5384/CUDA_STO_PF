## Strength ladder

- Direct registered observation: `reports`, `shows`, `exhibits`.
- Controlled three-realization comparison: `indicates`, `reveals within the examined realizations`, `is comparatively robust`.
- Frozen-model relation: `governs within the V2 contract`, `sets the modeled signal`.
- Processing proposal: `motivates`, `makes testable`, `could extend`.
- Reserved until new evidence: `proves`, `validates`, `controls`, `generalizes`, `quantitatively reproduces`.

Every use of `determine`, `govern`, `select`, `predict` or `validate` must carry its domain: object class, transport contract, cases, temperature and data role.
