# Recommended target journal V3

## Two separate judgments

- **Scientific story maturity:** coherence, novelty and mechanistic value of the pathway–outcome separation.
- **Evidence closure/submission readiness:** authority, object identity, thermodynamic identity, finite size, transport identity and reproducibility.

The reframe raises story maturity; it does not automatically close evidence.

## Objective comparator result

The idea is comparable to the audited Acta/npj papers at the conceptual level because it does more than report coarsening: it separates the variables selecting a microstructural pathway from those setting a property consequence. Its closest Acta comparators are A08 for bounded mechanism inference, A01 for post-nucleation handoff, A06 for observation/data-role discipline and A09 for spatially resolved causality. It is currently weaker than A02/A03/A06/A08/A09 in decisive evidence: no forward processing loop, no implemented observation operator with independent configuration, no designed spatial causal field and no frozen production-authority three-case analysis. Its closest npj comparator is N03 in contract discipline and N01/N04 in workflow sequencing; it is weaker in archive/transfer closure but stronger than a generic workflow paper in having a concrete material punchline.

## Can the punchline close the paper?

Yes as the **scientific closing statement**, provided it is written as contract-bounded and the production numbers are not presented as final. No as a **submission-closing proof** today. The phrase “interfacial-area loss determines how the thermal window closes” must mean “within the frozen V2 interface-scattering model”; experimental total-κ closure and between-PSD/design causality remain open.

## Recommended route

1. Keep an Acta-oriented manuscript architecture because the strongest novelty is a materials mechanism.
2. Do not submit to Acta until the P0 authority/operator/thermodynamic/thermal-identity gates close; the P1 finite-size bridge and at least one designed spatial or processing test are also needed for the strongest causal interpretation.
3. If no new experiment or designed control is feasible, target Computational Materials Science after the minimum P0 closure, or Physical Review Materials if the mechanism analysis becomes rigorous and compact.
4. Target npj only if the versioned operator, completed scalable workflow, transfer contract and public archive become first-class evidence.
