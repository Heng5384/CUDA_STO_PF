# Spatial causality writing plan V3

## Current formal role of A/B/C and Case 001–003

`fixed-PSD spatial uncertainty propagation and thermal-response robustness test`

The current evidence supports random-realization variability. It does not yet support designed spatial control, a universal spatial law or an ensemble-converged distribution.

## Three levels

1. **PSD defines the size hierarchy.** Initial radii define the available curvature hierarchy, donors/receivers and large-tail potential. Quantified between-PSD effects remain pending the 21-case design.
2. **Spatial organization selects the survival pathway.** Neighbours, surface gaps, capture zones, local density, elastic interactions and size–position assignment bias survival and final `M6`.
3. **Interfacial-area loss sets the modeled thermal outcome.** Under V2, similar `Sv` loss produces similar interface-driven recovery even when the large tail differs.

Core sentence: “Variables controlling the coarsening pathway are not identical to those controlling its thermal consequence.”

## Designed spatial architectures required next

| Architecture | Frozen PSD/inventory? | Intended contrast | Minimum response |
|---|---:|---|---|
| clustered | yes | short gaps/high local density | survival, merge, `Sv`, `M6`, Δκ |
| uniform | yes | narrow capture-zone distribution | same |
| large-particle-separated | yes | suppress large–large competition | large-tail rank and survival |
| size–position-correlated | yes | place large particles in large/small capture zones | isolate hierarchy×space interaction |
| strain-biased | yes | align particles with external/internal elastic bias | morphology, local energy, survival, `Sv` |

Each architecture needs multiple seeds and must be compared against the random baseline at identical PSD, inventory, volume fraction, thermodynamics and numerics.

## Local descriptor–fate analysis

Extract initial nearest-neighbour center distance, surface gap, local number density, Voronoi/capture-zone volume, pair correlation, size–neighbour correlation, local elastic energy, local chemical potential, graph degree and local large/small imbalance. Responses are dissolution time, survival, growth rate, merge probability, final rank and solute capture.

Use size/PSD/density controls, case random effects, cross-validation and finite-size checks. Upgrade from `selects` to a named causal descriptor only when effect direction and uncertainty are stable under designed contrasts.
