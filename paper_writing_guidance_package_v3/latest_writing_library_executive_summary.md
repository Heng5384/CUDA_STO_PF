The novelty is not continued coarsening itself. It is the separation of three controls: PSD defines size hierarchy, spatial organization selects survival, and interfacial-area loss sets the leading modeled thermal consequence. This is conceptually comparable to Acta/npj mechanism/workflow papers, but the present evidence is weaker than the strongest precedents in observation-operator validation, designed causal controls and production closure.
