# Section-by-section writing guide V3

| Section | Question answered | Core statement | Evidence status | Must not claim |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Introduction | What finite-lifetime state needs explanation? | Sheskin's interface-rich 6 h state evolves toward higher κ at 48 h | SUPPORTED framing | highest-zT window |
| Data roles | Which dataset does what? | Sheskin defines 6→48 h κ/microstructure; Yu resolves broader AQ→48 h defects | SUPPORTED | one merged ageing trajectory |
| Thermodynamics | What driving-force contract is used? | calibrated pseudo-binary model; production used legacy runtime | PARTIAL/BLOCKED final | validated phase diagram; exact contract used in old runs |
| Initial states | What is reconstructed? | experimentally admissible resolved-β ensemble, not a unique real state | SUPPORTED method logic | recovered true 6 h seed |
| PF Methods | What physics and scope? | globally conserved coherent-elastic post-nucleation propagation | SUPPORTED_WITHIN_CONTRACT | absolute nucleation/full precipitation history |
| Observation operator | How do model objects become observables? | blur→threshold→components/watershed→cutoff→2D/3D sampling | PENDING IMPLEMENTATION | quantitative APT/PF validation |
| Design | What does Case 001–003 isolate? | fixed PSD/inventory/physics; random centers and radius–position assignment | CONDITIONAL | designed spatial control |
| Structural Results | What changes? | survival and large tail are spatially sensitive | CONDITIONAL | ensemble convergence |
| Thermal Results | What is robust? | V2 interface-driven recovery varies about 3.3% of mean Δκ across three cases | CONDITIONAL_WITHIN_V2 | all transport is space-insensitive |
| Descriptor Results | What information matters? | `Sv+M6` compresses tested full-PSD transport better than `Nv+Rmean` or `Sv` alone | SUPPORTED; full PSD authority | `M6` uniquely determines κ |
| Discussion | What is the conceptual advance? | pathway variables differ from thermal-consequence variables | PARTIAL/CONDITIONAL | universal causal law |
| Processing | What can be tested? | thermal history and elastic bias may extend window lifetime | PROPOSED | already demonstrated stabilization |
| Conclusion | What should readers retain? | target the lifetime of the interface-rich state, not exact coordinates | SAFE implication | ready processing recipe |

Paragraph pattern: topic claim → traceable evidence/figure → mechanism interpretation → evidence boundary → transition. Limitations must sit next to the affected inference, not only in a final generic paragraph.
