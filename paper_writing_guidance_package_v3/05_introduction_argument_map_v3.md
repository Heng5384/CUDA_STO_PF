## Six-paragraph Acta map

1. **Finite-lifetime state.** Sheskin's 6 h sample combines high Ag-rich object density with reduced κ; by 48 h the population coarsens and κ recovers. Define “window” as an interface-rich microstructure–transport state, not a marketing label or arbitrary interval.
2. **Why averages are insufficient.** Mean composition, mean radius or number density alone cannot encode the PSD tail, interfacial area or local competition network that evolve during coarsening.
3. **Observation gap.** APT, SEM/TEM and PF components detect different object classes and sizes. A unique PF-resolved β population at 6 h is therefore not identifiable from one dataset.
4. **Ensemble response.** Introduce experimentally admissible resolved-β ensembles constrained by inventory, matrix chemistry and observation windows, propagated by a conserved coherent-elastic PF model.
5. **Mechanism question.** Ask whether PSD and random spatial realization control the same aspects of evolution, and whether their structural sensitivity transfers directly to thermal response.
6. **Contribution and implication.** State the pathway–outcome separation and the processing target: extend the lifetime of the interface-rich state rather than reproduce unique coordinates.
